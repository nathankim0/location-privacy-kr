# Attributions

location-privacy-kr was designed by installing and running the projects below on real tasks (one mobile app and the open-source OwnTracks location-sharing app), then writing new rules where they agreed and ruling where they conflicted (`skills/location-privacy-kr/references/conflicts.md`). No text was copied. Ideas are credited here.

## Legal sources

The checklists paraphrase the Act on the Protection, Use, etc. of Location Information (위치정보의 보호 및 이용 등에 관한 법률), its Enforcement Decree, and the Personal Information Protection Act, as summarized in the public 2026 business-operator training material on location information protection by the Korea Media and Communications Commission and KISA. Statutes are public; no slide text or images were reproduced.
